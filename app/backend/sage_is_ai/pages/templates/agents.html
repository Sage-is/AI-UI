MOBILE-FIRST, which is the framework's rule and not a preference: the suffixes are `min-width` queries, so a base value written for a desktop and patched downward produces a broken phone layout. Base values here are the phone. Only three props earn a suffix — the toolbar direction at `-sm` and the grid columns at `-lg`/`-xl`. Everything else looks the same at every size, so repeating it in a suffix would be noise. The column counts are lifted from `Models.svelte` rather than re-decided. Don't reinvent what already works and what people already recognise. TWO CLASSES, both shared and both structural. `.btn` carries `:focus-visible` and `:hover`, which the prop vocabulary cannot express and without which keyboard users get no feedback at all. `.toast` fades itself with `@keyframes`, same reason. Everything else is props, so this surface adds ZERO lines to `pages.css` — the branding lesson, where 99 hand-written lines took a 31% cut down to 8%.
