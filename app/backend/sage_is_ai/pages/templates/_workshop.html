That came from Branding, where 99 hand-written CSS lines were written before anyone asked whether they were needed, and deleting them took the surface's cut from 8% to 31%. If a rule can be a prop, it is a prop. TWO CLASSES ONLY, both shared and both structural. `.btn` carries `:focus-visible` and `:hover`, which the prop vocabulary cannot express and without which keyboard users get no feedback at all. `.toast` fades itself with `@keyframes`.
